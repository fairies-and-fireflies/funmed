Services 

Read on for more information regarding the services we provide, then check out our health care plans for starting your journey.

Functional Medicine

This plan includes a visit to establish care that includes a 60 minute appointment where we dive deep to get to know you, your history, and your health journey.  Following this appointment, you will receive a detailed plan of care that could include more extensive lab work, personalized nutrition, nutritional supplements/medications, and lifestyle recommendations.  We will continue to work on implementing this plan with scheduled follow up visits as needed, a six to twelve month commitment is recommended.  Chronic disease does not occur overnight, therefore we feel you will have the most long term success in committing to partner with us to work on your health. 

Pediatric Functional Medicine

Children are not "tiny adults".  Managing the health of children in the integrative medicine space requires pediatric specific training in protocols, lab interpretation, and supplement dosing that are specific to the needs of this population.  Our pediatric practitioner, Joy Badillo, has devoted special training to understanding the needs of children from an integrative lens.  She actively participates in a mentor/membership program made up of practitioners who are pioneering the field of pediatric integrative medicine led by one of the leading experts in pediatric functional medicine, Dr Elisa Song of "Healthy Kids Happy Kids".  

Menopause Wellness

Menopause is a normal physiological process.  However, suffering and the decline in health that frequently coincide with this process are not!  

This option will include a 60 minute visit and history focused specifically on hormone health.  You will then receive a personalized plan of care that may include nutritional supplements, more detailed lab testing, a personalized food plan and lifestyle recommendations.  Additionally, body identical prescription and compounded hormone replacement therapy is available if recommended for you.  This plan's  monthly membership includes one year of refills for any prescriptions as well as periodic 30 minute check in appointments as needed.  

Supporting hormone health can be absolutely life changing for so many women!  Don't wait to make this investment for your health! 

Annual Wellness

Want a functional medicine perspective on wellness?  Interested in a deep dive into bio markers for optimizing your physiology?  Curious what lifestyle habits are the most important for longevity?  Our annual wellness services are the perfect solution!  If you are someone who is not experiencing significant health issues and would like to keep it that way, come see us once a year for an optimizing wellness visit!!

This will include a 60 minute visit focused on health promotion and wellness optimization.  This does not take the place of a visit with your primary care provider, but is meant to add a deeper dive into lab work and health habits that could impact your health long term.  This package for ultra wellness could include recommendations for supplements, detailed lab investigation, a personalized food plan, and lifestyle recommendations. 

Pediatric Wellness

Want a unique approach to your well child visits from the lens of a pediatric functional medicine practitioner?  Want to be able to ask all those day to day kid questions to someone with a holistic view?  Want to know what to do when your child is sick and how to really help their little bodies fight off the crud?  This plan was created with you in mind.  This plan has limited availability and is currently on a waitlist, please contact us for more details. 

Health Coaching

Our practice also offers services through a Functional Medicine Certified Health Coach. This provides our clients with structured, personalized support to help them implement and sustain lifestyle changes. This includes: Food-as-Medicine meal planning and elimination diet guidance; stress, sleep, and movement optimization, pantry and product overhauls (clean food, beauty, and home), gentle, functional movement coaching for strength, flexibility, and resilience, and behavioral support with accountability in alignment with clinical care plan.
